Reporting period. Feeds are collected every six hours, but the report is published once a week, on Monday morning, and describes the whole preceding Monday–Sunday week. Every figure on this page is a full-week figure — with one exception, stated below — and no partial week is ever published. Buzz for a candidate is the sum, over panel texts mentioning that candidate during the week, of the candidate's influence (normalized betweenness centrality, Eq. 1) in each text's Centering Resonance Analysis network. Share is within chamber. Senate, House and Governor are ranked separately and each section's percentages are shares of that chamber's buzz, summing to 100% across the 24 displayed races. A 20% Senate share and a 20% House share are different quantities; do not compare across sections. The denominator is the displayed races only, so it changes when the roster changes. Tone* = mean document sentiment (AFINN-165) of mentioning texts — coverage tone, not stance toward the candidate; grayed when under 5 mentions in the week. Centrality Index = a candidate's share of chamber buzz ÷ share of chamber mentions (classified from 3 mentions): CENTRAL (≥1.25×) = structurally central to the texts that mention them; PERIPHERAL (≤0.8×) = mentioned often but in passing; between = balanced. Mud Meter. The heading is the 2008 original; the number is the Negative Framing Index (NFI, W-D20) = mean AFINN negativity of the words directly co-occurring with a candidate's OPPONENT in that candidate's own campaign press releases, shown from 3 releases. NFI is the one exception to the weekly rule (O-W21a): it keeps a trailing 14-day window, because a 7-day window leaves too few releases to average. It therefore overlaps the previous report and is not a description of the report week alone. It measures what a campaign says, not what the press says — it is not coverage tone and it is not stance. Adjacency is not predication: the metric cannot distinguish attacking an opponent from denouncing something on the opponent's behalf. Two alternative ring weightings are stored alongside the published figure for a sensitivity check. A dashed chip marks a press index not yet confirmed free of third-party clips. PILOT COVERAGE: campaign releases are collected for only 0 of the 24 displayed races. A missing NFI means no measurable corpus, NOT a clean campaign. Race selection: chambers ranked independently, 8 displayed each; independent polling averages where ≥2 recent nonpartisan polls exist, else consensus handicapper ratings, ties broken by 2024 presidential margin. Polls with an undisclosed sponsor, and polls testing candidates who are not yet nominees, are excluded from the averages. Buzz measures attention structure in 65 monitored sources balanced left/center/right — it is not a forecast.

Please cite: Corman, S., Kuhn, T., McPhee, R., & Dooley, K. (2002). Studying complex discursive systems: Centering Resonance Analysis of organizational communication. Human Communication Research, 28(2), 157–206.
